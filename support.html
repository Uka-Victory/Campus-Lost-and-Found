Account Help

Login and Password Help

Use the forgot password link on the login page to request a password reset email.

  • Enter the same email address used during registration.
  • Check inbox and spam folder for the reset email.
  • If your email changed, update it from Settings after login.
Lost Item Reports

Reporting a Lost Item

Give clear details like category, date lost, location, description, and unique marks.

  • Use unique marks to improve matching.
  • Be specific about where you last saw the item.
  • Only open reports should remain unresolved.
Found Item Reports

Reporting a Found Item

State where the item was found and where it was handed in.

  • Write identifying details carefully.
  • Use private verification notes for admin-only checks.
  • Keep the report accurate so matching works better.
Claim Help

Claim Verification

Claims should be submitted through your own lost report, then matched with a found report.

  • Choose your lost report first.
  • Then choose a matching found report.
  • Give proof details honestly and clearly.
Notifications

Possible Match Alerts

The system can notify users when similar lost and found items appear.

  • Check your dashboard notifications often.
  • Matching is helpful but not perfect.
  • Admins still review important claim actions.
Passport Upload

Passport Image

Passport upload is optional for now.

  • You can still register and log in without it.
  • You can update it later from Settings.
  • If upload fails, it should not stop normal use of the portal.
Need More Help?

Practical Next Steps

If something looks wrong in the portal, gather the page name and what happened before asking for help.

  • Say the exact page where the issue happened.
  • Say what you expected to happen.
  • Say what actually happened instead.
