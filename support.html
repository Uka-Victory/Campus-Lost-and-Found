Support Center

Guidelines for reporting items, verifying claims, automated matching, and account recovery.

Account Help

Login and Password Help

Use the forgot password link on the login page to request a reset email.

  • Use the email address registered on the portal.
  • Check your inbox and spam folders for recovery links.
  • Update email addresses or passwords anytime in Settings.
Lost Item Reports

Reporting a Lost Item

Provide comprehensive details to improve algorithmic matching precision.

  • State clear categories, locations, and approximate loss dates.
  • Include unique identifying marks (stickers, serial numbers, wallpapers).
Found Item Reports

Reporting a Found Item

Help restore items while keeping sensitive identifiers confidential.

  • Describe items clearly without exposing private verification details.
  • Log private admin notes and exact hand-in custody locations.
Claim Help

Claim Verification

Claims must link your active lost report with a matching found report.

  • Select your active lost report first.
  • Choose an eligible matched found report.
  • Provide proof of ownership for manual admin verification.
Notifications

Match & Decision Alerts

The system notifies you of automated suggestions and claim verification status.

  • Clicking match notifications opens the claim filing form.
  • Approved claims provide instructions for physical item recovery.
Privacy & Security

Item Data Protection

Public listings conceal sensitive item details to prevent fraudulent claims.

  • Only administrators can view full custody and unique mark logs.
  • Resolved items are removed from public searches automatically.
